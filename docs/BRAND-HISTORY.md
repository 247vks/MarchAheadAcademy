## Useful brand heritage

- The name **March Ahead Academy** is prominent and established.
- The central group of aspirants communicates collective progress and preparation.
- The sunrise motif suggests momentum and opportunity.
- The circular academy emblem may inform future identity work after its source, ownership, and reproducibility are confirmed.
- Warm gold/orange is part of the historical visual language, but should be refined into the current navy, ivory, and restrained antique-gold system.

## Original emblem concept v1

`assets/brand-concepts/three-services-one-path-emblem-v1.png`

Concept: three service-coloured paths—Army olive, Navy deep blue, and Air Force blue—converge into one upward direction inside an open compass form. The mark contains no vehicles, weapons, flags, official insignia, or copied stock elements.

Status: **concept only, not yet approved as the production logo**. The image generator embedded its transparency checkerboard into the bitmap despite a targeted background-removal pass. It must be properly vectorised and optically refined before use in the website header, favicon, printing, or embroidery.
