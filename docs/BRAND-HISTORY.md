## Useful brand heritage

- The name **March Ahead Academy** is prominent and established.
- The central group of aspirants communicates collective progress and preparation.
- The sunrise motif suggests momentum and opportunity.
- The circular academy emblem may inform future identity work after its source, ownership, and reproducibility are confirmed.
- Warm gold/orange is part of the historical visual language, but should be refined into the current navy, ivory, and restrained antique-gold system.

## Logo exploration

### Iconography emblem v2 — concept

The concept at `assets/brand-concepts/iconography-emblem-v2.png` explores a recognisable, non-weapon visual language:

- Army: upward rank chevrons, discipline, and progression.
- Navy: an anchor cue and ocean wave.
- Air Force: open wings and a sky arc.
- Academy: a central compass for credible career guidance.

The attached military-icon screenshots were used only as references for clarity and line weight. No official service insignia or individual reference icon was copied. This is an approval-stage concept and has not replaced the live website identity. A final production mark should be redrawn as a clean SVG after the direction is approved.
