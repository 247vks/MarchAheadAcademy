MARCH AHEAD ACADEMY — SHORT EXPERT INTERVIEW BRIEF
Prepared 12 September 2026. Private working document; not website copy.

Commander Sharma: please start with Topic 1. Five to ten bullet points or a
short voice note is enough. There is no need to write a finished article.
The other topics can follow separately, at your convenience.

Please speak from your experience, distinguish historical practice from
current official procedure, and omit confidential methods, candidate names
or identifiable cases. An invented example is fine if clearly labelled.
We will send the edited wording back to you before attributing it publicly.

TOPIC 1 — WHAT DOES AN SSB PSYCHOLOGIST ASSESS?
1. How would you explain the purpose of psychological assessment to a first-timer?
2. What do candidates commonly misunderstand about what their responses convey?
3. How would you explain consistency without suggesting all responses must sound alike?
4. What is useful self-awareness, and how can a candidate develop it in ordinary life?
5. What can and cannot responsibly be concluded from one practice response?
Destination: improve the existing article, not a new competing URL:
https://www.marchaheadacademy.com/ssb-psychology/what-does-an-ssb-psychologist-assess/

TOPIC 2 — CAN YOU PREPARE FOR SSB PSYCHOLOGY TESTS?
1. Which abilities can preparation genuinely improve?
2. Where is the boundary between familiarisation and rehearsing a performance?
3. What would a useful first week of preparation include?
4. What makes feedback useful, and what would make it misleading?
5. How should someone decide what to practise next rather than simply practise more?
Destination: enrich the existing preparation guide:
https://www.marchaheadacademy.com/selection/ssb/psychology-tests/

TOPIC 3 — TAT: BEFORE PRACTISING
1. What should a candidate understand before working with a fresh image?
2. How can they distinguish what is visible from what they infer?
3. What makes an account coherent without prescribing a model storyline?
4. What errors arise from forcing a prepared story onto an unfamiliar image?
5. What simple review exercise would you recommend after independent practice?
Destination: https://www.marchaheadacademy.com/selection/ssb/tat/

TOPIC 4 — WAT: THINKING BEYOND POSITIVE SENTENCES
1. What do candidates misunderstand when they try to make every sentence positive?
2. How should a candidate attend to the actual word rather than a rehearsed sentence?
3. Which aspects of written expression can be practised usefully?
4. How would you review a practice set without assigning a supposed selection score?
5. What advice would you give someone worried that ordinary responses are not impressive?
Destination: https://www.marchaheadacademy.com/selection/ssb/wat/

TOPIC 5 — SRT: WHAT USEFUL PREPARATION MEANS
1. What should candidates notice first in an unfamiliar situation?
2. How should they consider practical constraints and other people involved?
3. What is the difference between a realistic action and a dramatic-sounding response?
4. Which preparation habits help someone express their thinking clearly?
5. What self-review questions would you suggest without supplying a model answer?
Destination: https://www.marchaheadacademy.com/selection/ssb/srt/

TOPIC 6 — PSYCHOLOGY PREPARATION FOR REPEAT CANDIDATES
1. How can a candidate review preparation without guessing why a board did not recommend them?
2. What information is useful to bring to an initial coaching conversation?
3. How can someone identify whether their practice has become overly rehearsed?
4. What evidence would show a meaningful change in preparation habits?
5. How can a candidate maintain perspective while preparing for another attempt?
Destination: https://www.marchaheadacademy.com/ssb-psychology/psychology-preparation-for-repeaters/

OPTIONAL FOLLOW-UP FOR EACH TOPIC
One misconception to correct; one practical exercise; one example you approve
for public use; one sentence you would most want a candidate to remember.
These are prompts, not conclusions the article has to reach.
