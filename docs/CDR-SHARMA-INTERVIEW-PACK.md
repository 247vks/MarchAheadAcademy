# Cdr Sharma authority interview pack

Purpose: capture original, accurately attributed insight from Cdr Sulakshan Kumar Sharma (Retd) for March Ahead Academy's candidate guidance. This is an editorial interview, not an attempt to obtain confidential selection material, scoring methods, recalled questions or guaranteed-answer formulas.

## Before recording

Confirm these choices in plain language:

- May the interview be recorded for transcription?
- May answers be edited for clarity without changing meaning?
- Should published observations use direct quotations, paraphrases, or both?
- Is the attribution "Cdr Sulakshan Kumar Sharma (Retd), Director, March Ahead Academy" accurate?
- Which parts, if any, should remain off the record?
- Cdr Sharma will review quotations and biographical claims before publication.

## Suggested format

- 60–90 minutes in one sitting, or two 40-minute sessions.
- Ask for real principles and anonymised examples, not candidate identities.
- Follow useful answers with: "What did that look like in practice?"
- Mark every answer as expert perspective, historical experience, or a fact requiring an official source.
- Do not publish operationally sensitive, confidential or personally identifying information.

## Part 1 — professional foundation

1. What originally led you toward military education and psychology?
2. Which teaching and training roles most shaped how you understand young candidates?
3. How did teaching cadets at the National Defence Academy differ from teaching elsewhere?
4. What did leading or planning large-scale sailor training teach you about learning under pressure?
5. How did your academic work in psychology, education and the sciences influence one another?
6. What responsibility should an academy accept when advising a candidate about a defence career?
7. What does "clarity before coaching" mean to you personally?

## Part 2 — understanding SSB and AFSB

8. What do candidates most often misunderstand about the purpose of an SSB?
9. How would you explain the relationship between psychology, group testing and interview without discussing confidential assessment methods?
10. Why is it misleading to reduce selection to tricks, keywords or a model personality?
11. What is the difference between becoming familiar with a process and rehearsing a performance?
12. What should a candidate hope to learn about themselves before attending?
13. Which uncertainties can responsible preparation reduce, and which must candidates accept?
14. What would you tell a parent who asks for a guaranteed formula?

## Part 3 — Stage I, OIR and PP&DT

15. What preparation habits genuinely support careful reasoning under time constraints?
16. How can candidates practise separating observation from inference?
17. What usually makes a group discussion productive?
18. What unhelpful behaviours arise when candidates equate volume with leadership?
19. How should someone respond when the group does not accept their interpretation?
20. What can a candidate learn from an unsuccessful practice discussion?

## Part 4 — psychological-test preparation

21. Without discussing confidential material, what is the purpose of written psychological assessment in broad terms?
22. Why can searching for an "ideal answer" be counterproductive?
23. How can candidates build a truthful record of responsibilities, decisions, setbacks and learning?
24. What is the difference between healthy reflection and manufacturing a favourable image?
25. What writing or attention habits can candidates safely practise?
26. Which common myths about psychological testing would you most like to correct?
27. What should an ethical coach refuse to promise or provide?

## Part 5 — group testing

28. What does useful participation in a group look like?
29. How can initiative coexist with listening and adaptability?
30. What is the practical difference between leadership and domination?
31. What should a candidate do after their suggestion is rejected?
32. How can candidates review group practice without imitating visible gestures or jargon?
33. What role do sustainable fitness and composure play in preparation?

## Part 6 — personal interview

34. What should candidates know accurately about their own history and application?
35. What makes career motivation sound informed rather than borrowed?
36. How should a candidate discuss setbacks or weaknesses honestly?
37. What communication habits make an answer clear and proportionate?
38. What should candidates do when they genuinely do not know an answer?
39. What are the most common signs of excessive rehearsal?
40. How can a mentor provide feedback without writing a personality for the candidate?

## Part 7 — candidate and parent guidance

41. What questions do candidates repeatedly ask too late?
42. What questions should parents ask before paying for preparation?
43. How should someone decide whether a defence career fits their values and circumstances?
44. What habits can a Class 10 or Class 12 student begin without prematurely choosing one entry?
45. What should a graduate do before investing in examination or SSB preparation?
46. How should candidates respond constructively to non-recommendation?
47. What does responsible service mean beyond clearing an examination?

## Part 8 — closing reflections

48. Which piece of advice has remained consistent across your years of teaching?
49. What has changed most in young candidates and their preparation environment?
50. What do online preparation businesses often get wrong?
51. What do you want a March Ahead Academy reader to feel and do after reading your guidance?
52. Is there an important question we have not asked?
