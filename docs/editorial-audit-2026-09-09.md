# Public-copy editorial audit — 9 September 2026

## Result and method

The problem is real and extends beyond eligibility. The strongest issues are internal publishing-status copy, unsubstantiated monitoring/review promises, and a shared SSB byline that can imply article-specific expert involvement. This is a read-only source audit: no website copy, schema, configuration or deployment was changed. This report is the only new artifact. No live rendered-page visual audit or factual eligibility revalidation was performed.

All `app`, `lib` and `components` TypeScript/TSX/JSON files were screened with targeted searches for workflow, verification, future publishing, review, pending/draft, qualification and prescriptive wording. Matches were read in context; principal affected pages and shared templates were inspected directly. UI-library CSS/classes and runtime errors were excluded as non-editorial material. This is a site-wide copy screen plus contextual review, not a claim that every rendered sentence received an independent expert fact-check.

Severity: **P1** trust or unsupported operational/provenance implication; **P2** clear internal-workflow leakage or prominently awkward visitor copy; **P3** polish or repetition. Proposed wording is a recommendation, not implemented. Academy facts supplied by the owner are not disputed.

## Retain: genuine safeguards, not workflow leakage

- Entry-specific date-of-birth, subject, qualification, intake and document requirements; different branches and commission frameworks are not interchangeable.
- Only authorised medical assessment determines service medical eligibility; general exercise advice is not medical clearance.
- The concise global independence/notification statement, as requested by the owner.
- Official-source links and meaningful source dates. Do not remove citations to make copy warmer.
- Clear labelling of original practice questions, especially where a visitor could mistake them for official material.
- No guaranteed recommendation; do not infer an assessor’s decision from an isolated sentence. Keep this where it answers a real question rather than in every CTA.
- The cookie/privacy explanation about future collection versus past data is functional user information, not leaked workflow.
- Private in-person address remains private. Online coaching and appointment-only visits are confirmed.
- Owner-supplied credentials including 57 years teaching experience remain unchanged.
