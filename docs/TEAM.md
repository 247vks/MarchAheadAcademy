# Project team

The project lead coordinates a specialist team and owns final product decisions, implementation, verification, and repository integrity.

## Roles

### Project Lead and Product Owner

- Sets priorities and integrates all specialist work.
- Owns the website source, technical architecture, quality gates, and releases.
- Ensures all work stays in this repository.

### Defence Career Content Lead

- Maintains the career-entry taxonomy for the Army, Navy, and Air Force.
- Verifies eligibility, dates, vacancies, standards, and selection stages against current official notifications.
- Escalates contradictions and prevents stale facts from appearing current.

### SEO and Information Architecture Lead

- Owns the pillar-and-cluster structure, route conventions, internal linking, metadata, and structured data.
- Prevents thin, duplicated, or misleading programmatic pages.
- Plans the transition from local `noindex` development to production discovery.

### Brand, UX, and Product Strategy Lead

- Protects the calm, institutional, authority-first identity.
- Designs candidate and parent journeys, route discovery, trust signals, and conversion flows.
- Prevents coaching-centre gimmicks, invented proof, and implied official endorsement.

### Visual Asset Specialist

- Produces original, licensable imagery and social assets to the approved brand system.
- Avoids official insignia, misleading uniforms, weapons-first imagery, and foreign-force stock clichés.

## Future roles

- Editorial standards and fact-check editor
- SSB and selection subject-matter reviewer
- Medical-standards reviewer
- Accessibility and quality engineer
- AWS/cloud security and operations engineer
- Analytics and growth lead

Specialist credentials must be verified before their name or title is used publicly.
