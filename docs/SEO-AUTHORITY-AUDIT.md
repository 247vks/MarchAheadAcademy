# SEO and authority audit — 6 September 2026

## Positioning opportunity

March Ahead Academy can become more useful than high-volume coaching sites by being the clearest independent guide to Indian defence careers: precise official sourcing, plain-language interpretation, and identifiable expert insight. The differentiator should be evidence and judgment—not volume, urgency, or selection claims.

## Current gaps

- No visible author/reviewer system, page version history, correction path, or claim-level citations.
- Important guides link to broad official portals instead of the exact notice or page supporting a claim.
- Several pages are introductory rather than comprehensive enough to satisfy a candidate’s full question.
- No sitemap, structured data, canonical strategy, breadcrumbs, or systematic FAQ coverage yet.
- The notification tracker is currently a directory of sources rather than a dated, maintained update ledger.

Keeping the local site excluded from indexing is correct. Indexing should only be enabled when the public domain, canonical URLs, contact details, and launch content are ready.

## Competitive review

The review considered [SSBCrack](https://www.ssbcrack.com/), [Testbook](https://testbook.com/), [NCA Academy](https://ncaacademy.com/), [Cavalier India](https://www.cavalierindia.com/), and [SSB Counselling](https://www.ssbcounselling.com/). Their common strengths are content scale, current-exam coverage, course visibility, and broad keyword targeting. Common weaknesses are repetitive pages, sales-first journeys, shallow source attribution, and limited transparency about who interpreted the information.

March Ahead should not imitate their publishing volume initially. It should win on source precision, transparent authorship, expert context, and decision-quality content.

## Authority architecture

### Core hubs

- Service careers: Army, Navy, Air Force, and later Coast Guard.
- Examination routes: NDA, CDS, AFCAT, and relevant service-specific entries.
- Selection: SSB stages, psychology, GTO, interview, medicals, and merit.
- Eligibility: age, education, marital status, physical/medical standards, and women’s entries.
- Preparation: learn, lead, prepare, communicate, train, and serve.

Each hub should link to focused supporting pages and every supporting page should link back to its hub and adjacent official source.

## Editorial trust system

Every substantive guide should show:

- Author and expert contributor.
- Last reviewed date based on an actual review, not a global hardcoded date.
- Exact official sources beside the claims they support.
- A short “March Ahead perspective” box clearly separated from official facts.
- A corrections link and a concise change log.
- A plain disclaimer that the governing notification controls and no selection is guaranteed.

## 90-day content programme

### Days 1–15: trust and technical foundation

- Add sitemap, robots rules for launch, canonical metadata, breadcrumbs, Organization/Person/Article structured data, and reusable source/corrections components.
- Publish the complete Cdr Sharma profile and editorial ownership page.

### Days 16–30: deepen the highest-intent guides

- Expand NDA, CDS, AFCAT, and SSB pages with route comparisons, exact citations, FAQs, expert notes, and contextual next steps.
- Separate evergreen explanations from current-cycle dates and vacancies.

### Days 31–60: build entry and eligibility clusters

- Cover service-specific officer entries, NCC routes, women’s entries, medical standards, document preparation, and age/education comparisons.

### Days 61–90: useful tools and parent guidance

- Add a notification-led eligibility explorer, entry comparison tool, glossary, parent guide, and a maintained update ledger.
- Publish fewer, stronger articles answering real candidate questions and link them into the relevant hubs.

## Measures of authority

- Percentage of factual claims with adjacent official citations.
- Guides with named author, expert contribution, and actual review date.
- Organic visits landing on non-home pages.
- Movement from guide to related guide or career-path comparison.
- Qualified call, email, and WhatsApp enquiries originating from educational pages.
