# Authority operating system

## Mission

Become the most reliable independent place to understand, compare, verify, and prepare for careers in the Indian Army, Indian Navy, and Indian Air Force.

Authority is earned through verifiable accuracy, original expert insight, transparent corrections, useful decision tools, and sustained publication quality.

## Active authority squad

### Official Defence Source Intelligence Lead

- Monitors official notifications, corrigenda, deadlines, results, and recruitment portals.
- Maintains the source registry and flags conflicts or stale information.
- Prevents old intake data from appearing as current guidance.

### Cornerstone Content Editor

- Owns the initial authority cluster and page briefs.
- Separates evergreen explanation from intake-specific facts.
- Coordinates expert-insight prompts for Commander Sharma.

### Editorial Trust and Fact-Checking Lead

- Owns source hierarchy, fact-level citation requirements, review cadence, correction rules, and publication gates.
- Requires independent verification of high-risk facts.
- Enforces testimonial, advertising, disclosure, and independence standards.

The Project Lead integrates the work, approves publication, and owns implementation.

### Source precedence

1. Current intake notification and its corrigenda
2. Official recruitment portal current notice
3. UPSC or stable service page
4. Ministry of Defence or PIB for policy context
5. Reputable secondary context only when no decision-critical fact depends on it

Search snippets, coaching sites, and AI-generated answers are never evidence.

## Fact record required for decision-critical claims

- issuing authority and document title
- entry and cycle/intake
- notification number and publication date, when available
- exact page, paragraph, clause, or table
- official URL and access timestamp
- verifier and review date
- status: open, closed, corrected, superseded, or verification in progress
- local archive/checksum where lawful and practical

If official sources conflict, publish **Official sources conflict—verification in progress** and stop calculators from issuing a definitive result.

## Review cadence

| Volatility | Examples | Review |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Live | Applications, deadlines, vacancies, corrigenda and results | Every working day; more frequently in the final 72 hours |
| High | Intake eligibility, DOB windows, fees, medical and physical standards | Every official update and at least every 30 days |
| Medium | Evergreen entry and selection overviews | Every 90 days |
| Low | Study methods and labelled expert commentary | Every 12 months |

Overdue live facts must be removed from active-status surfaces or visibly marked as unverified.

## Publication gate

A factual page cannot be published until:

- the exact examination, entry, and intake are identified;
- the current notification and corrigenda have been checked;
- every decision-critical claim has an adjacent official citation;
- dates include year, time, and time zone when applicable;
- official facts and academy guidance are visibly distinguished;
- a named author, fact-checker, and qualified reviewer are shown;
- the last review, next review, version, and correction link are present;
- no guarantee, government-affiliation implication, or unsupported superlative appears;
- calculators include boundary and incomplete-input tests;
- medical interpretations have qualified review or remain faithful official summaries;
- testimonial/result evidence and written consent are on file;
- AI assistance is disclosed when substantial;
- mobile, accessibility, and plain-language checks pass.

## Core independence statement

March Ahead Academy is an independent education and career-guidance organisation. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, authorised by, or part of the Ministry of Defence, the Indian Army, Indian Navy, Indian Air Force, Indian Coast Guard, UPSC, or another government body. It does not influence recruitment, selection, medical assessment, or merit and does not guarantee selection. The applicable official notification and recruitment authority always control.

## Claims prohibited without exceptional independent substantiation

- guaranteed selection or medical clearance
- “100% success”, “No. 1”, “best”, or “sure-shot” claims
- predicted cut-offs presented as facts
- privileged access to selection boards or recruitment authorities
- definitive eligibility when facts are missing or intake rules are not versioned
- implying that all roles or branches are open to every candidate
